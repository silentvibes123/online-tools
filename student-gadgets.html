What to Look For When Buying Student Gadgets

A quick guide so you don't waste money on the wrong products

Buying a Mouse for Study / Office Work

  • For general use, 800–1600 DPI is enough. High DPI (12800+) is only needed for gaming or large screens.
  • Wireless is convenient but needs charging. Wired never runs out of battery — prefer wired for exams/lab work.
  • Check if it works on both Windows and Mac if you switch between devices.
  • Avoid very cheap mice (below ₹200) — sensor accuracy is poor and they fail quickly.

Buying a Laptop Stand

  • The main benefit is eye-level alignment — this reduces neck strain during long study sessions.
  • Look for height adjustability. Fixed-height stands may not suit everyone's desk setup.
  • Check compatibility with your laptop size (most stands fit 13"–17").
  • If you use your laptop on bed or floor, a non-slip base is essential.
  • Foldable/portable stands are better for hostel students who move frequently.

Buying a Cleaning Kit

  • A dusty keyboard/laptop fan can overheat the device — cleaning extends laptop life significantly.
  • Look for kits with both a brush and a cleaning solution — dry brush alone is not enough for screen cleaning.
  • Microfiber cloth is essential for screens — avoid paper towels (they scratch).
  • Clean your laptop every 2–3 months. A basic kit under ₹300 is all you need.
