Why Compress Images?

  • Government exam portals (SSC, UPSC, Railway) have strict file size limits — usually 20KB to 100KB
  • Email attachments have size limits — compressed images are easier to send
  • Website images load faster when compressed — better user experience and SEO
  • WhatsApp and Telegram have upload limits for documents
  • Compressed images save storage space on your phone and computer

Tips to Get Best Results

  • Start with a high-quality original image — compressing an already blurry photo will make it worse
  • JPG format compresses better than PNG for photos
  • For signatures, PNG with white background works best
  • If the compressed image looks too blurry, try a slightly higher target size
  • Crop unnecessary white space around the photo before compressing

Frequently Asked Questions

Will compressing reduce image quality?

Some quality reduction is normal when compressing to very small sizes like 20KB. However, for exam portals the quality is usually acceptable. The tool tries to maintain the best possible quality at your target size.

Can I compress PNG images?

Yes. The tool supports JPG, PNG, and WEBP formats. PNG files are generally larger than JPG, so they may need more compression to reach small sizes like 20KB.

Is there a file size limit for uploading?

No server-side limit since everything runs in your browser. Very large files (above 10MB) may take a few seconds to process.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Currently the tool processes one image at a time for best accuracy. You can compress multiple images one after another quickly.
