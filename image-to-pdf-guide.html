Tips for Best PDF Quality

  • Scan documents at 150-200 DPI for a good balance of quality and file size
  • Use good lighting when photographing documents with your phone
  • Crop images to remove unnecessary borders before converting
  • For text documents, black and white scans produce smaller PDF files
  • If the final PDF is too large, compress your images first using our Image Compressor
