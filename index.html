What is SwiftTool Pro?

SwiftTool Pro is a free, browser-based digital toolkit designed for students, professionals, and everyday users across India. It provides 23+ utility tools that work entirely within your web browser — no file uploads, no account creation, and no hidden charges.

Unlike traditional online tools that send your files to remote servers for processing, SwiftTool Pro uses modern browser technologies like the HTML5 Canvas API, PDF.js, PDF-lib, and Tesseract.js to process everything locally on your device. This means your sensitive documents — Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, salary slips, exam photos — never leave your computer or phone.

Free GST Calculator for Indian Businesses

India's Goods and Services Tax (GST) system requires businesses to calculate tax at rates of 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% depending on the product or service. Our GST Calculator makes this instant — enter the amount, select the rate, and get the CGST, SGST, and total amount immediately. Image to Text (OCR) — Extract Text from Any Photo

Our Image to Text converter uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology powered by Tesseract.js to extract text from any image. The extracted text can be copied or downloaded as a .txt file.
