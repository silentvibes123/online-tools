Frequently Asked Questions

Does it count Hindi or other Indian language text?

Yes. The word counter works with any language including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and other Indian languages written in Unicode.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. All counting happens in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server and is not stored anywhere.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average reading speed of 200-250 words per minute, which is the typical speed for most adults.
