UPSC & SSC Exam Word Count Tips

UPSC Mains Essay & GS Answer Word Limits
  • Essay Paper: 1000–1200 words recommended. Examiners prefer well-structured essays over padding.
  • GS Answer (10 marks): 150–200 words. Do NOT exceed — it signals poor filtering of relevant content.
  • GS Answer (15 marks): 250–300 words with sub-headings if needed.
  • Do headings count? Yes — every word on the page counts including headings, bullets, and conclusions.
  • Do diagrams count? No — diagrams/maps are not counted in word limits.
SSC & Other Competitive Exam Writing Tips
  • SSC Descriptive (Letter/Essay): Usually 200–250 words. Going over by 10–15% is generally acceptable.
  • Word count vs quality: A 200-word answer with 3 strong points beats a 300-word answer with filler.
  • How to check during exam: Practice counting 10 lines of your handwriting to know your average words per line. Most people write 8–12 words per line.
Practical tip: Type your answer draft here first, check the word count, then refine before writing in your answer sheet. This saves time during practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it count Hindi or other Indian language text?

Yes. The word counter works with any language including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and other Indian languages written in Unicode.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. All counting happens in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server and is not stored anywhere.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average reading speed of 200-250 words per minute, which is the typical speed for most adults.

Does word count include headings and bullet points?

Yes — every word in the text box is counted, including headings, sub-headings, and bullet point text. This matches how UPSC and SSC evaluators count — every written word on the page counts toward your word limit.

How do I count words in a typed Hindi answer?

Paste your Devanagari Hindi text directly — the tool counts each space-separated unit as one word, same as English. For mixed Hindi-English text, both are counted correctly.
