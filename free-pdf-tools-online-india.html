Why Browser-Based PDF Tools Are Better

Many online PDF tools upload your files to their servers for processing. This raises genuine privacy concerns for Indian users who handle sensitive documents:

  • Private documents (Aadhaar, PAN, salary slips) can end up on external servers
  • Free plans often have file size limits and daily usage limits
  • Watermarks are added to output files on free tiers

onlineTools processes everything in your browser using PDF-lib and PDF.js — two open-source JavaScript libraries. Your files never leave your device. No limits, no watermarks, no charges.
