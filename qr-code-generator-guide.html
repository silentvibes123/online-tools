How to Create a UPI Payment QR Code

To create a QR code for UPI payments, you need to enter your UPI ID in a specific format:

UPI QR Code Format:
upi://pay?pa=yourname@upi&pn=Your+Name&cu=INR

Example:
upi://pay?pa=manav@okaxis&pn=Manav+Katrodiya&cu=INR

Paste this in the QR generator and download. Anyone who scans it will be taken directly to the UPI payment screen.
Tip: You can also add a fixed amount to your UPI QR code by adding &am=100 at the end (replace 100 with your amount). This is useful for fixed-price products.

QR Code Best Practices

  • Test before printing — always scan your QR code with your phone before printing it on banners or cards
  • Use high resolution — download at the largest size available for print use
  • Keep good contrast — dark QR code on white background scans best; avoid light colors on light backgrounds
  • Leave white space — keep a white border (quiet zone) around the QR code for reliable scanning
  • Short URLs scan faster — if linking to a website, use a short URL for a less complex QR code

Frequently Asked Questions

Are QR codes free to create?

Yes. Basic QR codes for URLs, text, and UPI are completely free to create and use forever. You do not need to pay for a subscription.

Do QR codes expire?

Static QR codes (like the ones generated here) never expire. They will work as long as the URL or content they point to is active.

Can I edit a QR code after creating it?

Static QR codes cannot be edited after creation. If you need to change the destination, you must generate a new QR code. For editable QR codes, you need a dynamic QR code service.

How do I scan a QR code on my phone?

On most Android and iPhone devices, simply open the Camera app and point it at the QR code. A notification will appear with the link. You can also use Google Lens or any QR scanner app.

What is the maximum amount of text a QR code can hold?

A QR code can hold up to about 4,000 characters of text. However, more text means a more complex QR code that may be harder to scan. Keep it short for best results.
