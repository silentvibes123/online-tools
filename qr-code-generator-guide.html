Frequently Asked Questions

Are QR codes generated here free forever?

Yes, completely free. Unlike many QR code services that expire your QR after 30 days, QR codes generated here are static and work forever.

Can I edit a QR code after creating it?

Static QR codes cannot be edited after creation. If you need to change the content, simply generate a new QR code with the updated information.

What size should I print a QR code?

For reliable scanning, print QR codes at minimum 2cm x 2cm. For shop displays, 5cm x 5cm or larger is recommended. The downloaded PNG is high resolution and can be printed at any size.

Do QR codes work without internet?

The QR code itself works offline — scanning it reads the encoded data. However, if the QR contains a URL, the user needs internet to open that link.
