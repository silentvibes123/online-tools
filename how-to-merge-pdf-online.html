Merging PDFs for Govt Job Applications — Size Limits, Document Order & Error Fixes

Everything SSC, UPSC, Railway and bank applicants need to know before submitting a merged PDF

6 min read  ·  April 2026

You merged your PDF. You uploaded it to the portal. And then — "Invalid file" or "File size exceeded." This happens to thousands of applicants every year. This guide covers not just how to merge PDFs, but what actually goes wrong on government job portals and how to fix it before the deadline.

Most Common Reason for PDF Rejection: Merged file is too large, document order is wrong, or the file contains a password-protected page. Read this guide before uploading.

Portal-Specific PDF Size Limits (2026)

Always check the official notification for exact limits. These are the most commonly observed limits:

SSC (Staff Selection Commission)Max 500KB – 1MB per document
UPSC (Civil Services)Max 500KB per file
Railway (RRB / RRC)Max 2MB merged
IBPS / SBI Bank JobsMax 2MB total
State PSC PortalsVaries — usually 2MB to 5MB
NTA (JEE/NEET admissions)Max 500KB per document
Tip: If your merged PDF exceeds the limit, compress your images to 20–50KB before converting them to PDF. Use our Exam Photo Resizer for photos and Image Compressor for other images.

Correct Document Order for Govt Job Applications

Most portals do not specify order, but reviewers expect documents in a logical sequence. Wrong order does not usually cause rejection but can slow down document verification. Standard order:

1
Application form / admit card — Always first
2
Passport size photo — If required separately as a page
3
Photo ID proof — Aadhaar, PAN, or Voter ID
4
10th marksheet — Date of birth proof
5
12th / Graduation marksheets — In chronological order
6
Category certificate — OBC/SC/ST if applicable
7
Other certificates — Experience, NOC, disability, etc.

How to Handle Password-Protected PDFs

If one of your documents (like a bank statement or e-certificate) is password-protected, it cannot be merged directly. Two ways to remove the password:

Method 1 — Print to PDF (Recommended):
  1. Open the password-protected PDF in Chrome browser
  2. Enter the password when prompted
  3. Press Ctrl + P (Print)
  4. In the Printer dropdown, select "Save as PDF"
  5. Click Save — the new PDF has no password
Method 2 — Print on Mobile (Android):
Open PDF in any viewer → Tap Share → Print → Save as PDF. The exported copy will not have a password.

Scanned Documents vs Digital PDFs — What's the Difference?

This matters because scanned PDFs are much larger in file size:

Digital PDF (text-based)Very small — 50KB to 200KB
Scanned at 300 DPI (color)Large — 500KB to 3MB per page
Scanned at 150 DPI (grayscale)Acceptable — 100KB to 400KB
Scanned at 150 DPI (black & white)Best — 50KB to 150KB per page
For government document submission: Scan at 150 DPI in black and white for text documents. Use color only for photos. This alone can reduce your merged PDF size by 60–70%.

Common Upload Errors and Fixes

"File size exceeds limit"
Before merging: compress each image using our Image Compressor, scan documents at 150 DPI in grayscale, and remove blank pages from scanned PDFs using our Remove Pages tool.
"Invalid file format" or "Corrupted file"
Some portals reject PDF version 1.7+. Re-save your merged PDF by printing to PDF from Chrome — this creates a compatible version. Also avoid merging a file that is already compressed with unusual encoding.
"Document not readable" after upload
Likely a password-protected page inside the merged file. Use the Print to PDF method above for each protected document before merging.
Portal hangs or shows "processing" forever
Your file may be too large for the portal's upload handler. Try splitting your documents into two separate PDFs if the portal allows multiple uploads, or further reduce individual image sizes.

File Naming Before You Merge

Before merging, rename your files clearly so you can identify them in the merger. Example:

  • 01_Application_Form.pdf
  • 02_Aadhaar_Card.pdf
  • 03_10th_Marksheet.pdf
  • 04_Graduation_Certificate.pdf
  • 05_OBC_Certificate.pdf

Numbered prefixes ensure files stay in the correct order when you select them all at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

My merged PDF is 4MB but the portal only accepts 2MB. What do I do?

Re-scan your documents at 150 DPI in grayscale. For photos, use the Exam Photo Resizer to bring them under 50KB before converting to PDF. This usually reduces merged file size by 60–75%.

Can I merge a PDF and a JPG/PNG directly?

Not directly. First convert your JPG or PNG to PDF using our Images to PDF tool, then merge all PDFs together.

Does the portal check document authenticity in the merged PDF?

Portals at the upload stage usually only validate file format and size. Document authenticity is checked at physical verification stage. Ensure all documents are legible and not cropped.

My PDF has blank pages after merging. How do I remove them?

Use our Remove PDF Pages tool to visually select and delete blank or unwanted pages from your merged PDF.

Which browser works best for merging large PDFs?

Chrome or Edge on desktop gives the best performance for large files because they allocate more RAM to browser tabs. Avoid merging files larger than 50MB each on mobile.
