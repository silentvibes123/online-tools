Is It Safe to Merge PDF Online?

This is the most common concern people have — and rightly so. Many online PDF tools upload your files to their servers, which means your personal documents, certificates, and sensitive information are stored on someone else's computer.

SwiftTool Pro is 100% browser-based. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server. All merging happens locally on your device using JavaScript. Once you close the tab, nothing is stored anywhere.

Common Problems When Merging PDFs

Problem: Merged PDF file size is too large
Solution: Before merging, compress your images using our Image Compressor tool. Convert images to PDF at a lower quality setting to reduce file size.
Problem: PDF pages are in wrong order
Solution: After uploading, drag and drop the files to rearrange them before clicking Merge.
Problem: Password-protected PDF cannot be merged
Solution: You need to remove the password protection first. Open the PDF in Chrome browser, print it as PDF — this creates an unprotected copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I merge more than 2 PDF files at once?

Yes. You can merge as many PDF files as you want in one go. Simply select all files at once or add them one by one.

Does merging PDF reduce quality?

No. Merging PDFs does not affect the quality of the content. Text, images, and formatting remain exactly the same in the merged file.

Can I merge PDF on mobile?

Yes. SwiftTool Pro works on all devices including Android and iPhone. Open the tool in your mobile browser and follow the same steps.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no strict file size limit since processing happens in your browser. However, very large files (above 100MB each) may be slow depending on your device's RAM.

Can I merge scanned documents?

Yes. Scanned documents saved as PDF can be merged just like any other PDF file. If you have scanned images (JPG/PNG), first convert them to PDF using our Images to PDF tool, then merge.
