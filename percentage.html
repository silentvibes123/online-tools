Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate percentage of marks?

Divide your marks by total marks and multiply by 100. Example: 450/500 × 100 = 90%.

How to calculate discount percentage?

Subtract sale price from original price, divide by original price, multiply by 100. Example: (1000-800)/1000 × 100 = 20% discount.
