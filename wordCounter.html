About This Tool

Our free Word Counter instantly counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and estimates reading time. Perfect for essays, articles, social media posts, and exam answers with word limits.

Common Uses

Check essay word count for UPSC, SSC descriptive exams
Count characters for Twitter (280) and Instagram (2200) posts
Estimate reading time for blog articles and reports
Verify word limits for job applications and cover letters

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the word limit for UPSC essay?

UPSC essay paper typically requires 1000-1200 words. SSC descriptive papers require 200-250 words. Always verify with the official notification.

Does it work with Hindi text?

Yes, the word counter works with Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and all Unicode languages.
