Every year, millions of Indian students apply for government jobs through SSC CGL, UPSC Civil Services, IBPS PO, SBI Clerk, and Railway NTPC. One of the most common problems they face is resizing their passport photo to the exact file size required by the exam portal — usually under 20KB for signatures and under 50KB for photos.

If you upload a photo that is too large, the portal rejects it. If you compress it too much, the quality becomes unacceptable. This guide shows you the easiest way to resize your photo to the exact size limit without losing quality.

Why Do Exam Portals Have File Size Limits?

Government exam portals like SSC's OTRS, UPSC's online application, and IBPS's registration portal have strict file size limits for two reasons:

  • Server storage: Millions of applications are submitted, so small file sizes save storage space
  • Upload speed: Many candidates in rural areas have slow internet — smaller files upload faster

The most common requirements are:

  • Passport photo: 20KB to 50KB, JPG format
  • Signature: 10KB to 20KB, JPG format
  • Thumb impression: 20KB to 50KB, JPG format

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Don't use MS Paint to resize — Paint often increases file size when you save as JPG. Use a proper compression tool.

❌ Don't use WhatsApp to send the photo to yourself — WhatsApp compresses images in a way that changes the format and may not meet portal requirements.

❌ Don't crop the photo too much — Exam portals require a specific aspect ratio (usually 3.5cm × 4.5cm). Only compress, don't crop.
