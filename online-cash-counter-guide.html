Tips for Accurate Cash Counting

  • Sort notes denomination-wise before counting — mix-ups cause errors
  • Count ₹500 and ₹2000 notes twice — high-value notes need double verification
  • Check for torn or soiled notes — banks may reject damaged notes above certain damage limits
  • For large cash amounts, count in batches of 100 notes per denomination
  • Print the receipt and keep it with your physical cash for audit purposes

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ₹2000 note still valid?

No. RBI withdrew ₹2000 notes from circulation in May 2023. They are no longer issued and most banks have stopped accepting them for exchange.

Can I use this for foreign currency?

This tool is designed specifically for Indian Rupee (INR) denominations. For foreign currency, you would need a separate currency converter tool.

Does the tool save my cash data?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Your financial data stays completely private.
