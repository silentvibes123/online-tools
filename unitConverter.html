Frequently Asked Questions

How many km in a mile?

1 mile = 1.60934 kilometers. 1 kilometer = 0.62137 miles.

How to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. Example: 100°C = (100 × 9/5) + 32 = 212°F.
