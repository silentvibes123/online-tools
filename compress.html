About This Tool

SwiftTool Pro's Image Compressor lets you reduce the file size of any JPG or PNG image to a specific target size in KB. This is especially useful for government exam portals like SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and Railway which require photos under 20KB or 50KB. All compression happens in your browser — your images are never uploaded to any server.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Upload your JPG or PNG image using the tool above
  2. Enter your target file size in KB (e.g. 20 for 20KB)
  3. Click Compress and wait a moment
  4. Download your compressed image instantly

Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP formats are supported.

Is there a file size limit?

No limit. Everything runs in your browser so there is no server-side restriction.

Will my image be uploaded to a server?

No. Your image never leaves your device. All processing is done locally in your browser.
