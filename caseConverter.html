Frequently Asked Questions

What is camelCase?

camelCase writes compound words with no spaces, capitalizing each word except the first. Example: myVariableName. Commonly used in JavaScript and Java.

What is snake_case?

snake_case uses underscores between words in lowercase. Example: my_variable_name. Commonly used in Python and database column names.
