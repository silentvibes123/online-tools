Our Story

onlineTools was created with one goal in mind — to give every Indian student and professional access to powerful digital tools without paying for expensive software or subscriptions.

Every year, millions of students in India struggle with simple tasks like resizing a photo to 20KB for an SSC exam, merging PDF documents for a job application, or calculating GST for a small business invoice. These tasks should be simple, free, and private.

That is why we built onlineTools — a collection of 23+ free tools that work entirely in your browser. No sign-up, no watermarks, no hidden charges, and most importantly — your files never leave your device.

Why Browser-Based Tools?

Most online tools upload your files to their servers for processing. This means your personal documents, certificates, and photos are stored on someone else's computer — creating privacy and security risks.

onlineTools uses modern browser APIs (like the File API, Canvas API, and PDF.js) to process everything locally on your device. This means faster processing, complete privacy, and it even works offline for most tools.
